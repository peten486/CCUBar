# Third-party attributions

## Menu-bar emoji

The 🤖 glyph shown in the menu bar and popover is rendered at runtime using
Apple's system emoji font (Apple Color Emoji). No Apple graphics are embedded
in or redistributed with this application.

## App icon

`Resources/AppIcon.icns` is generated at build time from the
`gauge.with.dots.needle.67percent` SF Symbol (bundled with macOS) over a
blue→green rounded-rect background drawn by
[`Scripts/generate_sf_symbol_icon.swift`](Scripts/generate_sf_symbol_icon.swift).
SF Symbols are provided by Apple under the Xcode/macOS licence and may be
used in shipping applications.
